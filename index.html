Formula help
  • Use + − * / ( ) like a calculator, e.g. PTS + REB + AST
  • IF(MIN > 20, PTS / MIN, 0) picks one result or another
  • ROUND(x, 2), AVERAGE(a, b, c), SUM(a, b), sqrt(x), max(a, b), min(a, b), abs(x)
  • Percentages like FG_PCT are on a 0–100 scale (46.4 means 46.4%)
  • You can use your own saved stats inside new formulas
  • Names can't have spaces. Use letters, numbers and underscores.

How the rules work

Under the cap: a team can take in salary up to the cap ($165.0M) without sending any back.

Over the cap: a team has to send out close to what it takes back.

Hard cap: a team that takes back extra salary can't go above the first apron ($209.0M) for the rest of the season. Combining contracts to match salary locks it at the second apron ($221.7M).

Picks: teams can't trade first-round picks in two straight years.
